Research Experience

  • Jan. 2024 - Dec. 2026
    Nonlinear Dynamics and Data-Driven Optimization of Electromechanical Energy Harvesters
    Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute
    Graduate research integrating applied mathematics, nonlinear dynamical systems, control theory, and scientific machine learning to analyze and optimize coupled electromechanical energy-harvesting systems.
    • Advisors: Shaowu Pan, Yuri Lvov, and Diana-Andra Borca-Tasciuc.
    • Analyzed energy conversion, nonlinear phenomena, mixing entropy, stability, and eigenvalue and eigenmode behavior using Lagrangian, Hamiltonian, and Newtonian mechanics.
    • Applied neural ordinary differential equations and universal differential equations to identify latent dynamics and augment first-principles models with data-driven corrections.
    • Used sparse system identification, including SINDy, to discover interpretable governing equations, identify missing physics, and refine hybrid gray-box models.
  • May 2024 - Dec. 2025
    IBM Thin Films Fabrication Research
    Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute
    IBM-sponsored graduate research on semiconductor thin-film processing and electrothermal characterization for next-generation semiconductor fabrication.
    • Advisor: Theodorian Borca-Tasciuc.
    • Processed wafers using photolithography, thickness metrology, electron-beam evaporation, and liftoff to fabricate thermal-conductivity test structures.
    • Used the 3-omega electrothermal method to characterize in-plane and cross-plane thin-film thermal transport.
    • Developed automated data-processing and parameter-estimation tools to identify material-performance trends.
  • Jan. 2020 - May 2023
    Electrostatic MEMS Energy Harvesting for Sensing Power
    Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute
    Analytical modeling, topology optimization, fabrication, and testing of an impact-based MEMS electrostatic frequency up-converter.
    • Advisor: Diana-Andra Borca-Tasciuc.
    • Derived models for viscous fluid damping, electrostatic coupling, and collision dynamics.
    • Applied Gaussian-process Bayesian optimization with Latin hypercube sampling to improve output voltage and identify geometric design trends.
    • Fabricated devices in a cleanroom and characterized performance through frequency sweeps, applied acceleration, and DC-voltage bias.
