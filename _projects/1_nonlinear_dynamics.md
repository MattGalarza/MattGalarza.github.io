## Overview

My theoretical research examines how coupling, damping, nonlinearity, and network architecture govern energy exchange in mechanical and electromechanical oscillator systems. The objective is to identify physically interpretable principles connecting modal structure, resonance, stability, and directed energy transport.

## Current themes

- Lagrangian, Hamiltonian, and port-Hamiltonian formulations of coupled systems
- Eigenvalue, eigenmode, and frequency-response analysis
- Nonlinear stability, bifurcations, impacts, and pull-in behavior
- Modal mixing and entropy-inspired measures of energy redistribution
- Topology and scaling in multi-oscillator networks

## Methods

The work combines analytical derivations with numerical continuation, time-domain simulation, parameter sweeps, and energy-based diagnostics in Julia, Python, MATLAB, and Mathematica.
