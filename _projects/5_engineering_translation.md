## Overview

My applied engineering work connects modeling and analysis to fabrication, sensing, experimental automation, and deployment. I am particularly interested in tools that remain reliable under real geometric, manufacturing, and measurement constraints.

## Experience

At GE Aerospace Research, I developed concepts for robotics-enabled inspection and dimensional metrology of aerospace systems. This work included compact inspection mechanisms, compliant interfaces, point-cloud processing, and deployment documentation for in-situ engine inspection.

My current experimental work extends that translation mindset to microfabricated thermal test structures, automated measurements, and reproducible parameter-estimation workflows.

## Methods and tools

SolidWorks, Siemens NX, ANSYS APDL, Altium, PolyWorks, AutoCAD, MATLAB/Simulink, Julia, and Python.
