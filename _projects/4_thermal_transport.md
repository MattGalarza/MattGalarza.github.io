## Overview

My thermal-transport research investigates semiconductor back-end-of-line materials and multilayer thin films. I fabricate and characterize test structures and use the 3-omega electrothermal method to estimate in-plane and cross-plane thermal conductivity and interfacial thermal resistance.

## Experimental and analytical work

- Cleanroom processing, photolithography, evaporation, and liftoff
- Nanoscale heater design and fabrication
- Automated electrical and thermal data reduction
- Sensitivity, identifiability, and uncertainty analysis
- Inverse estimation of anisotropic material properties

## Engineering objective

This work supports improved thermal characterization and compact modeling for advanced semiconductor packaging and three-dimensional integration.
