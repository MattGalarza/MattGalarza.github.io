## Overview

I use scientific machine learning and system identification to complement first-principles models when important physics are uncertain, unresolved, or difficult to observe directly. The goal is not only accurate prediction, but also interpretable discovery of missing interactions and physically meaningful model corrections.

## Current themes

- Sparse identification of nonlinear dynamics (SINDy)
- Neural ordinary differential equations and universal differential equations
- Reduced-order and closure modeling for dynamical systems
- Bayesian optimization for engineering design
- Physics-informed diagnostics and uncertainty-aware inference

## Approach

Data-driven components are embedded within a physical modeling workflow and evaluated against conservation laws, stability, dimensional consistency, and out-of-sample behavior. This keeps the learned model connected to the mechanisms that govern the system.
